Our Story

Built on a handshake.
Finished with an oath.

Classic Jack Construction began with a single 1,100 sq.ft home in Vandalur — and a promise that the family would always know what was being built, why, and at what cost. A decade later, that promise has carried us through 150+ homes across Chennai, Chengalpattu and beyond.

We are not a brokerage of subcontractors. Every project carries a dedicated project manager, a documented quality checklist and a payment schedule tied to real milestones — not schedules.
